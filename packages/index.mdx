---
title: Packages
description: Packages allow you to use only parts of Solana Kit that your application needs.
---

### Why?

- Smaller size
- Less room for error

These are the modules published with `@solana/kit` 8.6.0. `@solana/library`, `@solana/subscribables`, and several build-only packages from older lists are not published. The subscription helpers are `@solana/subscribable`.
