> **Status:** Encrypt.Trade is an early/stealth-stage project as of early 2026. No official SDK or public documentation has been found. The description below is based on the project's known intent.

Encrypt.Trade aims to bring **encrypted order flow** and private trading to Solana — shielding trade intent from MEV bots and front-runners before execution.

## The Problem It Solves

On public blockchains, every pending transaction is visible in the mempool. MEV bots can:
- Front-run large swaps by buying before you
- Sandwich your trade between two of theirs
- Extract value at your expense

Encrypt.Trade (and similar protocols) aim to hide your order details until execution is finalized.
